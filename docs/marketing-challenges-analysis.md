### AI & Content
8. **What AI does Aura use?** — Claude by Anthropic. One of the most capable AI models for content generation, with strong performance on brand voice, tone, and factual accuracy.

9. **Will AI-generated content sound robotic?** — No. Aura learns your brand voice — tone, formality, personality, target audience — and generates content that sounds like you wrote it. You can test this with the "Generate Sample" tool before publishing.

11. **What is GEO?** — Generative Engine Optimization. While SEO optimizes for Google search rankings, GEO optimizes for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews). 25% of search now happens through AI engines.

12. **Does Aura do traditional SEO too?** — Yes. Full on-page SEO scoring, keyword research, competitor gap analysis, content briefs, and ranking tracking. GEO is a companion to SEO, not a replacement.

14. **Does Aura integrate with my CRM?** — Yes. Two-way sync with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, GoHighLevel, and Zoho. Leads captured in Aura automatically sync to your CRM.

15. **Can I import data from my current tools?** — Yes. Migration tool supports import from Hootsuite, Buffer, Later, Sprout Social, HubSpot, WordPress, and CSV.
